The Last Day in Chennai; Memories Soaked in Heavy Rain 🌧️🥀

The day came when I had to leave Chennai. I was happy to be leaving, but I also had an unfinished feeling in my heart. I called to ask if you were coming. It rained heavily until ten, that night.

Do you remember how we all partied together on the last day? It poured that day. It felt as if nature itself was trying to keep us from parting. You may remember that day, or you may have forgotten it. Even now, thinking about it brings tears to my eyes. 🤔❤️

I had to leave early in the morning. The cab was due at five, and I had to start travelling by 5:30. I asked if you were coming, but didn’t call again because I thought you might be tired or busy. I couldn’t meet you on the last day either.

Sandhya came with me to the station. I had so much on my mind that day, but couldn’t tell anyone. Both handkerchiefs got soaked with tears, and I cried a lot on the train. I didn’t tell you this, but I sent you a message saying “i want to see you again” 💌; you replied.

“Don’t cry. Whenever u want, come to Chennai, u can come.” That gave me hope that we could meet again. But after that, you greatly reduced your calls and messages, and my heart broke again 💔

That last day was not just a goodbye; it was also the day I walked away from the little memories I wanted to keep safe in my heart. 🥀

I left, but a part of me stayed behind in Chennai. That part was you. You may never have known that someone was leaving that city with so many thoughts about you. I said nothing; I took only the memories with me and moved forward.

The Day Hope Made Me Cry for Eight Hours continuously when Bibas was came here🥀

After arriving in Kolkata, I had a small hope that when the last person came from Chennai, you might come with them. I thought I might suddenly get to see you in person one day.

When that last person (Bibas) arrived, I met them—but you didn’t come. My hope broke that day. You had never promised to come; I had created that expectation myself, and I had to bear the pain of it breaking too.

I cried continuously for about eight hours that day. I don’t blame you. I was trying to let go of the hope I had quietly nurtured inside myself.

Sometimes we become emotionally attached not only to a person, but also to the possibility of meeting them someday. Perhaps that happened to me too.

11

Even at Home, I Couldn’t Escape the Memories 🏠🥹

I thought that going home for a while would change my surroundings, and that being with my family would lessen my thoughts of you and give my mind some rest.

Last Monday at around eleven at night, tears streamed from both my eyes as I thought of you. My mother asked, “What happened?” I couldn’t tell her anything. I went to sleep without saying a word, but the same thoughts kept running through my mind.

I thought a change of place would change my mind too, but the memories came home with me. That is the truth of my feelings. Still, I know I must take care of my mind and life. ❤️‍🩹 I hope to recover from this pain over the next 2–3 years, I may never forget you for the rest of my life. ❤️

12

My Childhood Friend Who Supported Me Through a Difficult Time 🫂

After coming to Kolkata, when I had a small accident in the second week of August, I told a childhood friend about you and my emotional state. She is now a nurse. She helped me understand things, made time for me, and listened to me.

Sometimes she comforted me on video calls even at two in the morning. I was very sad and emotionally affected at that time. She reminded me that taking care of my life, health, and future was important too.

Her support meant a lot to me. Even after telling her everything, my memories of you didn’t disappear completely. I’m not saying this to diminish her help, but because some feelings take time to understand and work through.

The Accident Scar Is Still on My Left Hand 🩹

After coming to Kolkata, one day in the second week of August, I was on my way to the office. As usual, your face and name were on my mind. I didn’t notice a stone in my path and fell. I injured my left hand, and the scar is still there.

It happened because I wasn’t paying attention, and I don’t hold you responsible. When I see the scar, I remember that time when my attention was on you even while I was walking.

Thinking of you is my feeling; taking care of my body, life, and future is my responsibility. I’m trying to keep that in mind. ❤️‍🩹

I Invited You to Every Happy Moment Like a Family Friend 🫂🌸

I felt close to you like a family friend, and I still feel that affection. When I think of visiting a sacred place, I want to invite you too. When there’s a party, festival, or function, my heart wishes you could be there.

When you weren’t there, I would make an excuse and leave the party early. I’d think, “This moment would have been even better if you were here too.” Sometimes I would leave without making any memories, lost in that thought.

There isn’t a day when I don’t think of you, and many times my eyes fill with tears. I’m not saying this to blame you or make you responsible. I only want to explain why I wish to include you in my happiness.

Sharing the joy of a sacred place or festival is one way to express affection. Whether you come or not is up to your wishes and convenience, and I respect that. 🙏

“I invited you to Jagannath Puri and to see Kolkata’s Durga Puja because those places and moments are special to me. When I visit a beautiful or sacred place, I wish I could share that experience with you too.”
“My invitation doesn’t mean you have to come. I simply wish you could have a place in my happiness. I know you have your own work, responsibilities, plans, and wishes.”
“Sometimes, when you aren’t at a party, I don’t feel connected to it and make an excuse to leave. Later, I wonder if I should have stayed and made memories with my friends. I’m learning that even while missing someone, it’s important to enjoy life’s good moments. 🌸”
“Voice of the heart: Dude, first ask if she has time! Booking tickets in your imagination for every trip won’t make them real!” 😂❤️

Friends, Work, and a Similar-Sounding Name in Kolkata 🙂

I have many friends in Kolkata. We go out together, attend parties, and enjoy our time. I want to grow in my career and do good things for my family. Even while smiling and talking on the outside, I think of you at certain moments.

My RMG reporting person, Anandita, treats me well. She shares updates and gives advice about technologies, and sometimes helps with attendance matters too. Her name seems about 70% similar to “Anandhi,” so even at work, hearing her name reminds me of you.

But Anandita and you are different people. I’m not comparing anyone with anyone else; this is simply a memory that the similarity in names brings to mind.

From Bihar to Madurai—Two Cities, Two Languages, One Wish ❤️

I’m from Bihar, and you’re from Madurai in Tamil Nadu. Our cities, languages, and cultures may be different, but when we talk, English becomes a bridge between us.

I think my English communication has improved through talking with you. You may have helped me without realizing it. Along with my affection for you, I have good memories and have learned a few things too. I want to talk with you every day to improve my communication as well.
“There was a time when I hesitated even to talk to girls. Today, I’m trying to share so many things in my heart with you. This change means a lot to me.”

The Rameswaram Trip and My Unfulfilled Wish to Visit Madurai’s Meenakshi Temple 🙏🌸

When returning after visiting Rameswaram, I had a ticket as far as Man-Madurai and planned to go from there to Madurai. I wanted to visit Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai, but due to lack of time and exhaustion, I couldn’t go. That wish remained unfulfilled.

After we became friends, Madurai felt closer to me because you are from there. I thought I might visit again one day and pray at Meenakshi Amman Temple. But later, since we were in different places, meeting wasn’t easy.

“If you feel comfortable and it’s appropriate, try sending me a few memories related to Meenakshi Amman Temple and a small photo of yourself. I’d like to keep them as a keepsake to remember you by. 🙏❤️”
“I want to keep it as a keepsake because it would be a small reminder of our friendship and help us remember one another.”But please don’t feel any obligation or pressure. It’s completely fine if you can’t or don’t want to. Your wishes and comfort matter most. 🌷”

You Appear in My Dreams Too 🥀

Sometimes you appear in my dreams too. I imagine us sitting and talking together or going somewhere. In dreams, everything is easy -> no distance, no worries about time, and no need to rush away.

When I open my eyes in the morning, reality is waiting. You have your life, and I have mine. There is distance, responsibility, and limited time between us.

“Dude, you once found it hard even to talk to a girl for six seconds. Now you’re planning an entire trip in your dreams!” 😂

Dreaming about something and expecting another person to make it happen are two different things. You have your own life, dreams, and wishes. I want to share my feelings, while giving your feelings equal importance.

Why Do I Call You? 📱❤️

You may have wondered why I message or call you. The answer is simple—I like hearing your voice and seeing you. Even a short conversation can make my day happier.

I understand that it isn’t possible to talk all the time. You have work, responsibilities, and need time for yourself. Sometimes you may be able to reply to a message but not have time for a call. They aren’t the same thing.

I don’t consider your time something I’m entitled to. Wanting to hear your voice is my feeling, but your time and peace of mind are just as important.

Between hope and Acceptance

💔 When Efforts Go Unnoticed — For your feelings about travelling over 3,200 km while she cannot spare even 2–3 hours.

I am willing to travel for 4–5 days just for one meeting with you, covering a distance of more than 3,200 km to reach your city. Yet, you say that you cannot spare even 2–3 hours to meet me. Honestly, I feel that it’s not a lack of time, but a lack of interest. Because when someone truly wants to meet you, they find a way, even in the busiest of schedules. 💔

❤️ A Heartfelt Appreciation — For appreciating her humility, kindness, and down-to-earth nature.

But even then, you always take the time to answer all my stupied questions without your interest. That reflects your humility, your good character, and the fact that you are a genuinely down-to-earth person. I truly appreciate this quality of yours. ❤️

🍂 Leaving the Door Open, Letting Go of Expectations — For expressing your fading hope while leaving the decision to contact or meet you in her hands.

Now, my hope has almost reached zero. So, I think it’s time for me to stop expecting a meeting that may never happen. But if someday you genuinely feel like meeting me, you can always message or call me from your side. I’ll leave that decision entirely up to you. ❤️
